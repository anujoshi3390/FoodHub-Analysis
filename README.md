🍔 FoodHub Delivery Data Analysis

Context

With the rapid growth of restaurants in New York City, online food delivery has become a lifeline for students and busy professionals. FoodHub, a food aggregator platform, connects customers to a variety of restaurants via a single smartphone app. The platform manages order placement, assigns delivery personnel, and collects customer feedback, while earning revenue through a fixed commission per order.

This project analyzes historical order data from FoodHub to extract actionable insights on restaurant demand, cuisine popularity, order efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

Objective

The goal of this project is to analyze customer order data to help FoodHub:

Understand the popularity of various restaurants and cuisines
Assess food preparation and delivery times
Identify trends in customer behavior across weekdays and weekends
Optimize logistics and enhance customer experience

Data Description

The dataset consists of detailed information on food orders placed through the FoodHub app. Below is a breakdown of the key features:

Column Name	Description
order_id	Unique identifier for each order
customer_id	ID of the customer who placed the order
restaurant_name	Name of the restaurant that prepared the food
cuisine_type	Type of cuisine ordered (e.g., Italian, Chinese)
cost_of_the_order	Total cost of the order in USD
day_of_the_week	Indicates if the order was placed on a weekday or weekend
rating	Rating (out of 5) given by the customer
food_preparation_time	Time (in minutes) taken by the restaurant to prepare the order
delivery_time	Time (in minutes) taken by the delivery person to deliver the order

Project Highlights

Analyzed most popular cuisines and restaurants by day type (weekday/weekend)
Measured average preparation and delivery times to assess bottlenecks
Explored customer satisfaction trends by cost and delivery performance
Visualized insights through charts and summary tables for clear storytelling
